# Kiro Login Guide

Quick reference for logging into Kiro.

## IDE & CLI Steps

1. Log into your AWS account (eg your sandbox)
2. In the Search bar, search for Kiro and goto the service
3. Click on Settings on the Kiro page
4. Copy the sign in url
5. Choose "Your organisation" when choosing a way to sign in
6. Choose use identity center
7. Paste the sign in url, eg https://d-90660xxxxx.awsapps.com/start
8. Specify the region, eg us-east-1
9. For username, go back to kiro settings
10. Click on the users & groups link
11. Under users, copy your username
12. Hopefully you know the password - hint check your vault!
13. MFA should also be in your vault

## CLI Steps

1. Start a terminal
2. Run kiro-cli --tui
3. A browser will open if not logged in
4. Follow above
